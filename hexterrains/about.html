    ℹ️ About Hex Terrains Framework

    Hex Terrains Framework is a high-performance terrain solution built entirely on Unity’s ECS (DOTS) architecture, using a hexagonal grid.
    It powers one of the core systems of my own upcoming game — so you can be confident that it will continue to receive regular updates and long-term support.

    This framework is designed to be as fast and lean as possible, leaving maximum headroom for your simulation logic within every frame.
    Whether you're building a strategy game, a city builder, or a simulation, Hex Terrains gives you a solid and scalable foundation to grow from.

    🎯 Vision

    The framework is built not just as a terrain renderer — but as a toolbox of powerful, modular systems:

    • 🧱 Hex terrain with multiple layers (heightmap, biomes, colors, etc.)
    • 🏗 Props, buildings, and unit support
    • ⚡ Chunk-based rendering and dirty update optimizations
    • 🎨 Lightweight rendering OR full ECS entities — your choice
    • 🧭 Optional systems like pathfinding and area overlays

    You only pay the performance cost for what you use.
